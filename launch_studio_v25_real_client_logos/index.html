GOOD TO KNOW08 / 09

Before we
press export.

How long does a video take?+

Most focused product films take around 2–3 weeks from kickoff to final delivery. Faster timelines can be scoped when needed.

Do you handle the script and concept?+

Yes. We can take the project from concept and script through edit, motion, sound and final delivery.

What do you need from us?+

Usually the product, brand assets, access to the relevant screens and a clear goal. We can help structure the rest.

Can you make multiple versions?+

Yes. We can deliver cutdowns, social versions, alternate aspect ratios and additional exports as part of the scope.
